Topic 01 Estimated Time: 20 Mins

From Data to Information

Core Concept: You live in a data-rich but information-poor world. Your fundamental goal in analytics is to transform raw noise into actionable insights.

  • • Data: Raw, unorganized facts—numbers or text without context (e.g., 100, Red, 10:04 AM).
  • • Information: Processed and structured data given explicit meaning (e.g., "100 customers bought the Red shirt at 10:04 AM").
  • • The DIKW Pyramid: As an analyst, you climb from Data → Information → Knowledge → Wisdom to discover patterns and prescribe actions.

Statistics

The "Why" Focus (Inference)

Heavy emphasis on understanding the underlying data generation process. Relies on strict mathematical assumptions, hypothesis tests, and confidence intervals.

  • ✓ High Interpretability
  • ✓ Strict Distribution Assumptions
  • ✓ Focus on Sample Inference
🤖

Machine Learning

The "What" Focus (Prediction)

Trains algorithms to find complex non-linear patterns. Minimizes empirical prediction errors on unseen test sets without strict theoretical assumptions.

  • ✓ High Predictive Accuracy
  • ✓ Handles Complex High-Dim Data
  • ✓ Empirical Generalization
Topic 04 Estimated Time: 30 Mins

Generalization as Search

Core Concept: Algorithms learn by searching through a massive Hypothesis Space to find the model that generalizes best to unseen future data.

The Bias-Variance Tradeoff:
  • Underfitting (High Bias): Model is too simple; fails on both training & future data.
  • Overfitting (High Variance): Model memorizes training noise; fails on future unseen data.
