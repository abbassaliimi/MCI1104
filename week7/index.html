1. Logistics: Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP)

Core Concept: Imagine managing a fleet of 500 delivery trucks across a metropolitan area. Forecasting traffic isn't enough; you must prescribe the exact sequence of turns, pickup order, and load balancing for every single driver.

  • • Vehicle Routing Problem (VRP): Moving beyond simple shortest-path algorithms, VRP prescribes optimal delivery routes across fleets while respecting hard constraints like capacity limits and time-window restrictions.
  • • Fuel & Carbon Minimization: By dynamically re-prescribing routes based on live traffic data and package priorities, logistics giants save millions of gallons of fuel annually.
